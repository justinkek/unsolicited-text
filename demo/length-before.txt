Great question! Let me walk you through
what is happening here.

So when you register a hook there are
really two layers at play. The first is
your own settings, which apply to every
session you start. The second is the
plugin's own registration, which travels
with it.

What is happening in your case is that
both layers are registering the same
script. This is a really common gotcha!

Essentially the harness does not
deduplicate across those two layers, so
the command just runs once per
registration.

To fix it you will want to remove one of
them. I would probably suggest removing
the one in your own settings, but
honestly either works depending on your
setup.

Let me know if you would like me to walk
through how to do that!
