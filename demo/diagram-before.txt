The turn end hook reads the reply you have just seen and counts its
lines of prose. If it ran over the ceiling, it writes a note to a
file named for the session, and shows you nothing at the time.

The next time you type anything, the prompt hook reads that file,
prints what it found into the session, and deletes it. So the
finding lands one turn after the reply it is about, and a session
you never write to again never reads its note.
