So, coming back to this after a while -
we covered a few options and it is worth
recapping where we landed, because there
were a couple of threads running at once.

We looked at one file against many, and
measured both. Reading the head was 3.5ms
against 15ms, and ten items cost 4KB
against 40KB, so neither was close to
mattering at the size we care about.

On the queue itself we settled on one
file per session rather than one for the
machine, which also took the concurrency
problem away entirely.
